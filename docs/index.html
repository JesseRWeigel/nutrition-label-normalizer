Nutrition label normalizer

Two products are only comparable once their labels are on the same basis. This page restates each label per 100 g, per serving, per 100 kcal and per container, and carries the label's own rounding as an interval instead of pretending the printed figure is exact.

Not medical or dietary advice. This tool cannot tell you whether a package is labelled correctly. It reads only what the panel says, so every figure here inherits the panel's own rounding, and a product that is mislabelled in an internally consistent way passes every check on this page.
